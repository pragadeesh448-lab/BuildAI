HOW TO USE THE PROTOTYPE

Your quick-start guide

Follow these steps to demonstrate the full BuildAI workflow to your faculty.

01
☷

Enter project requirements

Set your project location, budget, material quantities, delivery target and what matters most: cost, service score or speed.

02
⇄

Generate the supplier plan

BuildAI evaluates available demo offers and recommends which supplier should provide each material, including a reason for every choice.

03
⌖

Inspect supplier shops

Open each supplier to view their simulated product catalogue, indicative rates, sample availability and delivery estimates.

04
◇

Preview the finish

Try paint colours, tile patterns and wood finishes on a living-room photo-style preview before choosing materials.

05
⚡

Supplier Failure Recovery Engine

Strict Role Separation Workflow: When a supplier reports a disruption from their portal (stockout, transit delay, or partial shortfall), BuildAI captures the failure through the API, alerts the builder in real time, autonomously recalculates alternative supplier plans with cost and delay trade-offs, and awaits builder authorization before modifying any confirmed order.

Prototype data noticeSupplier inventories, prices, delivery performance and BuildAI service scores are simulated for demonstration. Scores are not real customer ratings. Confirm all real offers directly before purchasing.
